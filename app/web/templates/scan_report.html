Limitations

DocGuard performs static structural detection according to its configured, versioned policy — it does not execute or dynamically detonate documents. ALLOW does not guarantee that a document is benign; it means no risky characteristic covered by the configured detection model was observed. Incomplete analysis (encrypted, malformed, resource-limited, or timed-out input) fails closed and is never silently treated as ALLOW. This report reflects DocGuard's persisted result for this scan under the policy version shown above — it is a read-only presentation of that stored evidence, generated at the time shown, and is not digitally signed or cryptographically tamper-evident in this release.
